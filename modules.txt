🧭 1. Core System Modules

These form the foundation of the system’s structure and workflow.

Module	Description
Dashboard	Role-based analytics dashboard with charts (patients, revenue, occupancy, etc.).
Authentication & Roles (ACL)	8 department roles: Admin, Doctor, Nurse, Receptionist, Accountant, Pharmacist, Lab Technician, Radiologist. Manage access control per module.
Multi-Hospital / Multi-Branch	Manage multiple branches or clinics under one system.
Multi-Language & Multi-Currency	Language localization (English, French, Swahili, Arabic, etc.) and currency auto-conversion.
Settings & System Config	Control system-wide options (logo, time zone, tax, currency, SMS/email setup).
Export / Import Module	Export everything to Excel, CSV, PDF. Import bulk data (patients, medicines, etc.).
Notifications & Reminders	Email + SMS alerts (appointments, payments, lab results).
🧑‍⚕️ 2. People & Role Management
Module	Description
Doctors Management	Doctor profiles, specialties, schedules, and OPD charges.
Doctors Departments	Categorize doctors under departments (Cardiology, Neurology, etc.).
Nurses Management	Track nurse details, schedules, and assigned wards.
Receptionists	Manage reception operations (check-in/out, visitor logs).
Pharmacists	Manage prescriptions, stock dispensing, and medicine audits.
Lab Technicians	Handle pathology and radiology requests and reporting.
Accountants	Manage payments, invoices, and financial reporting.
Case Handlers / Social Workers	Manage patient cases, transfers, and follow-ups.
Patients Management	Patient registration, demographics, history, and digital EMR (Electronic Medical Record).
🏥 3. Medical Operations Modules
Module	Description
Appointments	Online & offline bookings, approvals, and reminders (SMS/email).
OPD (Out-Patient Department)	Manage outpatient visits, doctor consultations, prescriptions.
IPD (In-Patient Department)	Manage patient admissions, discharge summaries, bed transfers.
Patient Admissions	Bed allocation, treatment plan, discharge billing.
Prescriptions Management	Create, print, and share prescriptions digitally.
Patient Diagnosis Categories & Reports	Store diagnostic test results, categories, and findings.
Operation Reports / Surgery Module	Track surgeries, surgeons, anesthesia, and outcomes.
Birth Reports & Death Reports	Record maternity and mortality data with certificate export.
Investigation Reports	Lab, radiology, pathology, and special investigations.
🧪 4. Laboratory & Diagnostic Modules
Module	Description
Pathology Categories & Tests	Manage pathology lab tests, rates, and reports.
Radiology Categories & Tests	X-rays, CT, MRI, Ultrasound reports with image upload.
Lab Requests & Reports	Automated lab test workflow with status tracking.
💊 5. Pharmacy & Inventory Modules
Module	Description
Medicines Management	Add/edit medicines, expiry tracking, and batch numbers.
Medicine Categories & Brands	Classify medicines by category and brand.
Pharmacy Billing	Sell medicines, generate receipts, manage stock levels.
Full Inventory Management	Supplies, surgical items, consumables, vendor management.
Packages Management	Create and manage treatment or service packages (e.g., Maternity Package).
🩸 6. Blood Bank & Donor Management
Module	Description
Blood Bank	Track blood stock, group, expiry, and availability.
Blood Donors	Donor details, history, donation schedule, and blood collection logs.
🚑 7. Ambulance & Emergency
Module	Description
Ambulance Management	Track ambulances, drivers, and vehicle types.
Ambulance Calls	Record requests, charges, mileage, and reports.
Emergency Admissions	Direct emergency entry and triage record keeping.
🛏️ 8. Bed & Ward Management
Module	Description
Beds Management	Add beds, wards, and room types.
Bed Assignments	Assign and reassign patients to beds.
Bed Visualization	Interactive ward map with bed occupancy status.
Bed Types & Status	Single, double, ICU, maternity, etc. with color-coded status.
💳 9. Finance & Billing
Module	Description
Accounts Management	Chart of accounts, bank, and cashbook management.
Accountants	Manage financial records and reconciliations.
Advance Payments	Handle deposits and prepayments.
Payments & Payment Reports	Track payments from OPD/IPD, pharmacy, lab, etc.
Billing & Invoices	Central billing for all departments.
Hospital Charges & Categories	Define charges by category (consultation, bed, lab, surgery).
Expenses Management	Expense categories, payables, petty cash.
Income Management	Capture hospital income streams.
Insurance Management	Patient insurance, claims, verification, and approvals.
Payrolls	Employee salary calculation, deductions, and payslips.
🧾 10. Reporting & Analytics
Module	Description
Payment Reports	Filter by patient, department, or date.
Revenue Analytics	Charts of daily/weekly/monthly revenue trends.
Patient Reports	Demographic and department-wise stats.
Doctor Performance Reports	Appointment and revenue contribution tracking.
Lab & Pharmacy Reports	Diagnostic and medicine reports.
Export All Data	PDF/Excel exports for compliance.
🧰 11. Utility & Support Modules
