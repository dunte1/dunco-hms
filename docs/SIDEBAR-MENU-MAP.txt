3. EXAMPLE: WHAT SUPER ADMIN SEES vs PATIENT
================================================================================

Super Admin (e2e.super@hospital.com) sees ALL 13 sections including:
  Roles & Permissions, Backup & Restore, Audit Trail, Break-Glass,
  Elliana D, My Work, Hospital Management, Finance, HR, CMS, Marketing

Patient (e2e.patient@hospital.com) sees almost NO staff menus:
  No Hospital Management, no Clinical, no Finance, no Admin, no Elliana.
  Portal is separate: /patient-portal/* (own session auth)

Receptionist sees front-desk menus (patients, appointments, queue)
  but NOT Roles & Permissions, Mortuary, or pharmacy inventory.
